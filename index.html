Pose it. Keyframe it. Draw on it. Export it.

Make stickman fights, walk cycles, memes and short films, then export MP4, GIF or PNG.

What is Stick?

Stick is a desktop and web app for stick figure animation. You drag a figure’s joints to pose it, add a frame, pose it again, and Stick fills the motion in between. It has a real timeline with layers, onion skin, camera moves, visual effects, brushes for hand-drawn frames, and a rig builder for custom characters.

It opens Pivot Animator (.piv, .stk) and Stick Nodes projects, so you can bring your old animations with you. Everything runs on your own computer: no account, no watermark, no uploads.

Features

Pose any joint

Drag hands and feet; inverse kinematics bends elbows and knees for you. Lock angles, stretch nodes and pose 3D parts in depth.

Keyframe and play

Add a frame, change the pose, hit play. Tweens, easing curves and real-time WebGPU playback keep long animations smooth.

A real animation timeline

Simple frame strip or Advanced timeline with layers, folders, keys, easing, playback ranges, onion skin and audio tracks.

Built-in effects

Glow, aura, shadow, blur, particles, energy beams, explosions, smoke and power-ups, keyframeable per frame.

Draw on any frame

Ink, brush pen, pencil, liner, marker, charcoal, airbrush, chalk, oil and more. Mix hand-drawn art with your rigs.

Build your own rigs

Make characters from lines, circles, polygons, text and images. Start from examples like gears, fans, crates and 3D shapes.

Every command, one search away

Press F1 or Ctrl+P to find any command. Every shortcut can be remapped.

Light or dark, in 27 languages

A dark theme by default, a light theme when you want it, adjustable UI scale and a touch layout for tablets.

Export anywhere

One-click presets for YouTube, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, X, chat GIFs, transparent PNG frames and 4K. Export MP4, WebM (with transparency), GIF, APNG, PNG / JPEG / SVG sequences, or editable .piv and .stk files.

FAQ

Is Stick free?

Yes. Stick is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 license. There is no account, no watermark and no paid tier.

Is Stick a Pivot Animator alternative?

Yes. Stick opens Pivot Animator projects (.piv) and figures (.stk), and can export them back. It adds a layered timeline, tweening, effects, drawing and a modern WebGPU renderer.

Can I open Stick Nodes projects on PC?

Yes. Stick imports Stick Nodes figures, movieclips and projects, so you can keep working on a Windows or Linux computer.

How do I make a stick figure animation?

Pose the figure by dragging its joints, press Add frame (A), change the pose, and press Space to play. Stick tweens the motion between frames. Then click Export and choose MP4, GIF or a preset like TikTok.

Does it work offline? Does it upload my animations?

It works fully offline. Your projects stay on your computer; nothing is uploaded.

Build from source

Requirements: Node.js 22+ and Python 3.11+.

git clone https://github.com/blixvip/stick.git
cd stick
npm ci
python -m pip install -r backend/requirements-fast.txt
npm start            # desktop app (Electron)
npm run serve        # or the editor in your browser: http://127.0.0.1:47831
